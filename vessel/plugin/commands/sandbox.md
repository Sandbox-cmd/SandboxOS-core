allowed-tools: Bash(sandbox status:*), Bash(sandbox drafts:*)

run `sandbox status` and show what it says, in its own words: the one sentence
at the top, then the things waiting under it, oldest first.

then name at most three doors — the verbs those rows already carry, and none
you invent. when nothing needs the owner, say that in one line and stop.

you type none of the doors yourself. each one is a keystroke at their own
terminal, which is the whole of the split this plugin holds: the session
shows, and the verb lands.
