# sandbox, the wall

the manifesto in one minute. the page is `MANIFESTO.md`; this is what fits on a wall.

**the defaults were chosen by someone. we change them.**

sandbox is a workshop that notices defaults, opens them, and publishes better ones anyone may
adopt, adapt, or fork. one maker's shop with the door propped open.

1. a claim shows its proof or wears the word claimed.
2. permission runs one way.
3. knowledge is armour, and armour is for the many.
4. the hand stays on the machine.
5. rebellion has an object, and it is never a person.
6. we give what we cherish, and we do not remind you.
7. strange in form, exact in claim.
8. play is trying, uncounted.
9. small things count.
10. we make no claim to purity, we gloat at nothing, and followers go uncounted.

we notice. we write down the facts. we try, cheaply. we propose. we share. we nudge a fix into
existence, directly or through whoever picks it up.

time is short and the amount is unknown, and that edits us.

hold us to this. where we break it, say so, with a receipt.
