# the manifesto folder

the founding text of sandbox, the organisation, parked in the tool's repository until it has a
home of its own. sandbox os is one tool; sandbox is the workshop and the practice. the pages here
are the practice's, and they will move.

status: draft 0.1, written 2026-09-29 for the maker's cold read. nothing here is ratified.

## what is here

- `MANIFESTO.md` the page: why, what we stand on, the tenets with what each refuses, how to catch
  us breaking them, how we work, what we make, who we are, how we live, what stays out, the close,
  and the reading under every line that leans on the book.
- `WALL.md` the same in under two hundred words: the sentence, the ten tenets, the close.
- `DECONSTRUCTION.md` the maker's raw thoughts taken apart into atoms, the tensions between them,
  how each was resolved, what the reading changed, and the questions still open.
- `NAME-AND-MARK.md` whether to keep the name, the candidates if it changes, and a reading of the
  three marks with a recommendation.
- `SOURCES.md` how the whole quran was read, the digest by theme, the text's severe side named, the
  human traditions, and the rules of the form the draft was held against.
- `research/` the receipts: eight readers' notes covering all 6,236 verses, the brief they
  followed, the pick list by chunk, and the two studies on the genre and the traditions.

## how it was made

the whole quran was read in the abdel haleem translation, in eight chunks by eight readers, with
three more translations on hand for cross-reading. every quotation was machine-checked against
the source. the manifesto genre and the traditions behind the maker's posture were studied from
memory, with every quotation marked verbatim or paraphrase. the maker's thoughts were deconstructed
before a line was drafted, and every page here passes the house voice lint in `engine/voicelint.py`
with zero gates.

## what to read first

`WALL.md`, then `MANIFESTO.md`, then the questions at the end of `DECONSTRUCTION.md`.
