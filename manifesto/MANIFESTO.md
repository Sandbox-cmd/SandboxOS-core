# sandbox

a manifesto. draft 0.1, 2026-09-29, written for the maker's cold read on a reading of one book
and a shelf of others. the name is a placeholder until the name question is settled.

**the defaults were chosen by someone. we change them.**

## ∵ because

i started sandbox at a kitchen table. the world was changing the people i love, and changing me,
and almost none of that change was something anyone at the table had chosen. it arrived as a
setting. a default is a decision somebody made once, for their own reasons, that you inherit
without being asked. most of what makes a person feel stuck is a default.

four gates stand between a person and what they could know or make: what they are told, what
they can operate, who they know, and what they can pay. every gate is made of information, and information
is most of what a failed attempt costs, which is why the people who can afford to try are the ones
who already hold it.

the machine that arrived this decade has two settings. on the first, it takes what people hand
over and makes a noise from it: a shape that sounds like a cow and answers nothing. on the second,
it is the strongest tool one person has ever held, and that setting sits behind expertise, so the
few who can wire it decide what it is for. the froth is everywhere and the metal stays with the
smiths.

the economy around it extracts. it is built to keep you paying, and it keeps you working for
things that are already dead, the way a crew keeps labouring for a king who died standing up,
until something small eats through his stick. the ones who figure it out mostly keep it. they ask
what they will be paid and are paid in access. they sit in the pathway and charge the toll.

none of this needs a villain, because a default defends itself as tradition: we saw our fathers
do it this way, and this is our time-honoured way of life. the people who keep a default are usually the people
it pays, and what it produces in everyone else is the feeling of a wide earth closing in.

so the first thing we rebel against is our own comfort. a comfort is a default too, and a
rebellion that begins at home cannot turn cruel abroad without becoming a lie.

## what we stand on

i am a monotheist. i read one book for its core message and take the rest as mine to reason out,
and i hold that you may do the same with anything, this page included. what i took from it is
below, in human words. the lines themselves are in the reading at the end of this page, so that
nothing here asks to be taken on faith by anyone who does not share mine.

- pushing back is not a revolt against the order. it is the order. if some were not driven back
  by others, the earth would be entirely corrupt.
- what every fallen society lacked was a few people of plain sense who said no while the rest
  enjoyed the plenty.
- time is short and the amount is unknown, and this simplifies everything, because there is no
  time left for costume.
- work is watched while it is being done, so craft is worship, and the instruction to the first
  armourer was to measure the links well.
- a claim shows its proof or it wears the word claimed. saying a thing and then leaving it undone
  is the failure the book hates most.
- nothing is owned. everything is held in trust and handed back to whom it belongs.
- to be given knowledge is to be under a pledge to make it known, and the ones who hide it know
  it as well as they know their own children.
- the froth on the flood disappears, and what is of benefit to people stays behind.
- a good word is a tree with a firm root, and a mustard seed's weight is never lost from the
  scales.
- under every harmful default there is a good one you were made with.

## ∴ therefore

sandbox is a workshop that notices defaults, opens them, and publishes better ones anyone may
adopt, adapt, or fork. it researches, publishes, builds, and releases. it is one maker's shop with
the door propped open.

### the tenets, and what each one refuses

1. **a claim shows its proof or wears the word claimed.** we refuse to grade our own homework,
   because a claim you cannot open is a narrative, and narratives are the first gate. every page we
   publish names the command, the file, or the number that opens it, and where none exists it says
   claimed.
2. **permission runs one way.** we ask nobody's permission to build, because the gate was only
   ever a setting, and we build inside the law, because being barred is a fact and never a licence. we ask the
   owner's permission to enter, every time, because the house is theirs and an empty house is a
   no. this is the whole of our anarchism and the whole of our manners.
3. **knowledge is armour, and armour is for the many.** we refuse to sit in the pathway and charge
   a toll, because the only thing that ever kept a people safe was being able to hold their own
   ground. what we figure out, we publish. what we make, we make forkable.
4. **the hand stays on the machine.** the machine drafts and the person lands, because a record
   written while you slept is nobody's record. the strong tool gets built with that gate designed
   in, so that it can be handed to people instead of kept from them.
5. **rebellion has an object, and it is never a person.** we rebel against defaults and against our
   own comfort. we build the better thing beside the old one and leave the old one standing,
   because the aim is the old enemy turned into a friend, and because the reformer who strikes is
   read as a tyrant by the very person he helped the day before. we expose mechanisms. we verify
   before we repeat. we leave people unexposed, because a person may be better than us.
6. **we give what we cherish, and we do not remind you.** the work done for a stranger who only
   ever meets the work is the work we are proudest of, the way a wall gets repaired in a town that
   refused the repairer bread, for two orphans unaware of what lay under it. a gift followed
   by a reminder is a gift cancelled.
7. **strange in form, exact in claim.** weird is allowed, because the corporate-clean is a costume
   and honest roughness is the opposite of slop. the joke used as cover is refused, because a joke
   at the reader's expense shrinks them, and the point of this place is to make them larger.
8. **play is trying, uncounted.** the gate is strict so that the play can be fearless. our play is
   the earnest kind: a room where a failed attempt costs a note and nothing more, so that small
   survivable bets can compound. distraction is the other setting's product, and we leave it
   there.
9. **small things count.** we ship the small true thing and let it compound, because an atom's
   weight registers on the scales and a rock struck once can give twelve springs.
10. **we make no claim to purity, we gloat at nothing, and followers go uncounted.** the first rebel
    rebelled because he thought he was better, and a workshop pleased with its numbers has already
    lost. we remember when we were few.

### how to catch us breaking it

a tenet nobody could break is decoration. for each one, this is what a stranger points at.

| the tenet | what you point at |
|---|---|
| a claim shows its proof | a page of ours with a claim and no command, file or number beside it, and no word claimed |
| permission runs one way | a tool of ours that writes anything durable without the owner's key, or a page of ours that waited for a gatekeeper's blessing |
| armour for the many | a thing we figured out and kept, or a release with no licence to fork |
| the hand stays on the machine | any mode, flag or setting that lets the machine land its own work |
| rebellion has an object | a person named and shamed in anything we publish, or an incumbent attacked where a better thing could have been built beside it |
| we give and do not remind | a release that asks for credit, or phones home, or breaks when the name is stripped off |
| strange in form, exact in claim | a joke at the reader's expense, or a weird thing whose claims cannot be opened |
| play is trying, uncounted | a playground that counts, or a ship that celebrates on red |
| small things count | a number on our pages that was stored instead of computed when shown |
| no purity, no gloating, no count | the sentence "we are better than them", a gloat, or a follower count on any surface of ours |

### how we work

the loop is the same every time. we notice a clunky default or a broken thing. we research it and
write down the facts before we write down opinions. we try something, cheaply, where a mistake
costs a note. we propose. we share. we nudge a fix into existence, directly when we can and through
whoever else picks it up when we cannot.

we work at two speeds. fast where a mistake is cheap, slow where a thing becomes durable, ships, or
carries someone's trust. we release in parts, at intervals, little by little, because that is how
a heart is strengthened and how a reading gets finished before it is recited. we write in the
reader's own language. we ask those who know before we explain, and we explain so that people
reflect. we say "i do not know" where it is true, and we test the sincerity of our own assertions
against what we shipped.

### what we make

research, publications, builds, releases, and blueprints. a blueprint is a thing that works with
our name stripped off it: plain files, nothing that phones home, and a licence that lets you fork.

sandbox os is the first blueprint, and it is only that: the operating system and the ai under the
work, running on your own machine, where nothing durable lands without your key. sandbox is the
organisation and the practice. the tool is one run of the practice.

the class of tool the powerful keep for themselves is a shared model of what exists and how it
relates, plus a harness that lets a machine act on that model with permission and an audit trail.
that is the class we build in public, with the gate designed in. why does only one company get an
ontology? because nobody built the public one. so we will, and one rock struck well gives twelve
springs, each group knowing its own.

### who we are

one maker, whose ships are the benchmark. a crew, hired on two words, strong and trustworthy, and
on nothing about their network or their funding. the people who use what ships. and the strangers
who receive something passed along and never learn where it came from, which is fine with us and
was designed in. nobody joins anything. the door stays open to whoever walks in, including the ones
the respectable call the worst sort, and we leave their past unvetted, because it is theirs.

### how we live

we sell craft, time, support, and made things. the knowledge is free at the point of use, and
there is no fee that indebts. the line between a business and an extraction is the line between
trade and usury, and we stay on the trade side of it. whatever we keep, we keep for a stated time
and then let go: the licence today is free for everything except competing, and it converts to
open in two years. we pay the ones who administer. we fund the quiet ones who do not ask. we hire
on the fairest contract we know: a stated term, an extension only of your own free will, and a
promise not to make things difficult.

### what stays out

- an account, a sync, a server, telemetry. a record kept on someone else's machine is theirs.
- the agent that works while you sleep and hands you a summary over coffee.
- a dashboard, a second face, a brand as a dependency.
- a sect. we fork tools and keep people whole.
- the rage. it gets removed from the heart before the work is called finished.
- the sentence "we are better than them".

### amending this page

this page binds until the maker amends it, and amending is rare. every change lands on the
record with its date and its reason, so the chronology stays visible and the newest word wins.

## the close

time is short and the amount is unknown, and we are grateful, because it edits us. when the anger
passes, the tablets get picked up again. the wing goes down over whoever came in. we walk at a
moderate pace, and we keep the voice down, because the ugliest of voices is the one that brays.

hold us to this. where we break it, say so, with a receipt, and we will put it right as far as we
can.
