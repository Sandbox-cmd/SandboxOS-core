# the name and the mark

the maker said the name is open and named three marks. this page weighs the name against a
rubric, ranks the candidates if it changes, reads each mark, and ends with one
recommendation. nothing here was checked against a trademark registry or a domain lookup,
because the network is closed in the room this was written in. those two checks come
before any decision.

## what a name has to do here

six tests, drawn from the maker's own rules and the tool's laws:

1. it says the thesis in a word a child knows. law 15 holds every surface a stranger meets
   to first-read plainness, and a name is the first surface.
2. it survives the stranger's mouth. a person who hears it once can spell it and say it,
   in english and in arabic.
3. it carries no neutered reading. a word that also means contained, sidelined or "for
   practice only" argues against the organisation every time it is spoken.
4. it can be found. a search for it lands here and a handle exists.
5. it yields a mark without a designer's help.
6. it keeps sandbox and sandbox os apart, which the maker asked for in so many words.

## the case for keeping sandbox

the word is the thesis. a sandbox is a place where a failed attempt costs almost nothing,
and the README's whole argument is that trying should become survivable. the fourth law
(free play) and the `play` verb already live inside it. every language with a playground
has the word. the tool, the repository, nine bodies of lineage and one signed manifest
carry it. and the thing the maker most wants to refuse, fear, sits in the word's shadow: a
sandbox is where a child is allowed to fail.

## the case against

the same word is everywhere and it means "not real". every developer has a sandbox
environment and every payment provider has a sandbox mode. a sandboxed process is a
contained one. "keep it in the sandbox" means keep it out of production. that is test 3 failed
outright, and for an organisation whose point is change in the real world it is the
sharpest objection on this page. the word is also unsearchable (test 4), it carries none of
the rebellion, the faith or the craft, and the collision with sandbox os is built into it
(test 6).

one fact lowers the stakes of the whole decision. the maker designs for the stranger who
receives a blueprint and never learns where it came from. a name matters less to an
organisation like that than to almost any other, because the work is meant to travel with
the name stripped off.

## the verdict on keeping it

keep the word for the thing it names exactly: a place where trying is free. the name's
weakness (it sounds like practice) is answered by what ships, and the manifesto can answer
it in one line. the collision with the tool is the real problem, and it has two fixes. the
organisation keeps sandbox and the tool takes a name or a strong qualifier of its own,
which is the cheaper move, since a tool's name lives in a repository and a shim while an
organisation's name lives in people's mouths. or the organisation takes a new name and the
tool keeps its own, which is what the maker offered. the candidates below are for the
second path.

## candidates, if the organisation's name changes

ranked. each row: the word, what it says, where the text carries it, how it scores, and
the one risk that could sink it. the verse references are what the readers report from the
text; where a reference is still to be confirmed it says so.

1. **bayan** (بيان). clear statement, clear expression. it is also the everyday arabic word
   for a manifesto or a communiqué, so the organisation would be named after the very
   document this study serves. the text says god "taught him bayan" (55:4, wording to be
   confirmed from the readers' notes), and the maker's phrase radical clarity is this word
   in two english ones. tests 1, 2, 5 and 6 pass; test 3 passes; test 4 is medium, since
   bayan is a common given name and a russian accordion. the risk: it reads as a person's
   name in half the arabic-speaking world.
2. **fitra** (فطرة). the original disposition, the state a person is made in before the
   world overwrites it (30:30). this is the deepest fit with the thesis: harmful defaults
   are what got written over the good one, and the organisation's work is to open the
   overwrite. tests 3, 5 and 6 pass; tests 1 and 2 are weak for a reader who never met the
   word, and a name that needs a gloss argues with law 15 even if a proper noun gets one
   gloss for life. the risk: it belongs to a religious register, and it collides with the
   name of an obligatory charity (zakat al-fitr) in the ears of the people who know it.
3. **qalam** (قلم). the pen. the text swears by it (68:1) and names it as the instrument
   god taught with (96:4). it carries the record, the note, the debt written down by a
   scribe (2:282). short and sayable. the risk: it is one of the most used names in arabic
   education and publishing, so test 4 fails on volume, and its cousin kalam means
   theology, which invites a confusion the organisation does not want.
4. **mizan** (ميزان). the balance (55:7-9, the tool's fifth axiom). justice, measurement,
   a count computed the moment it is shown. the risk: heavy for a playful organisation, and
   taken by a large publisher and many others.
5. **burhan** (برهان). proof. "bring your proof" is a refrain of the text (2:111 and
   elsewhere, to be confirmed), and a claim that wears its proof is the tool's third
   conviction. the risk: it is a first name before it is anything else.
6. **sanad** (سند). support, and the chain of transmission that gives a report its
   provenance, which makes it the closest arabic word to receipt. the risk: many companies
   in the gulf already carry it, and it says nothing of play.
7. **warsha** (ورشة). workshop, in plain arabic. exact for the thing and honest about its
   size, and that is the whole of what it carries. the risk: an english ear hears warsaw.
8. **sawdust**. the wildcard, and the strange one the maker's brief leaves room for. the
   tool's own charter says the record fills the way sawdust fills a shop. it is the
   byproduct of making, which is what the maker wants the record to be, and it is warm,
   small, and impossible to mistake for a corporation. the risk: it names a waste product,
   and a stranger will hear that before they hear the shop.
9. the english field otherwise: **bench** (humble, but benched means sidelined, the same
   fault as sandbox), **kiln** (where soft attempts harden into lessons, craft-only),
   **seed** (the mustard seed on the scales, 21:47, and the good tree, 14:24; heavily used),
   **commons** (true to the blueprint stance; political and crowded).

if the name changes, bayan first, for saying the thesis and the document in one word a
mouth can carry. fitra if the maker wants the deepest word and accepts the gloss. sawdust
if the maker wants the strangest true one.

## the marks

- **⧖ white hourglass** (U+29D6). it says the maker's own sentence: time is short and the
  amount is unknown (surah 103, and 31:34 where no soul knows in what land it will die).
  two problems. to the people most likely to meet it, an hourglass is the busy cursor, a
  machine asking you to wait, which is close to the opposite of the meaning wanted. and at
  small sizes it reads as a bowtie. it renders widely.
- **∴ therefore** (U+2234). a claim carrying its proof, in one glyph. the shape is three
  points, one above two: a small collective, or a person standing on a foundation. anyone
  who sat through school geometry reads it, and it has been in every font since the first
  unicode. its mirror **∵ because** (U+2235) is the reason under the rule, which is the
  house's own standing rule (a boundary carries its reason). the pair can mark the two
  halves of the method: because this default harms, therefore this blueprint. the risk:
  alone it reads cold, and a few agencies use it.
- **the prototyping symbol.** the message names it without pasting it, so this page has
  no codepoint to read. candidates for what was meant: ⧉ two joined squares
  (U+29C9), ⟁ a triangle inside a triangle (U+27C1), ◈ a diamond inside a diamond
  (U+25C8), or ⏣ the benzene ring with a circle (U+23E3). one paste settles it.
- **※ reference mark** (U+203B). in japanese typography it means "note this", and the
  method begins with noticing. it is playful and the record is made of notes. the risk: an
  english reader has never seen it, and some fonts lack it.
- **⁂ asterism** (U+2042). three small stars in a triangle, the old typographic mark for a
  section break. small things that add up, which is the tool's seventh conviction. the
  risk: it is decorative, and it renders badly where it renders at all.
- **◌ dotted circle** (U+25CC). the placeholder a font shows where a base character is
  missing: a setting waiting for a choice, which is the thesis drawn in one line. strange,
  and exact. the risk: it is a technical glyph and half the readers will think their font
  broke.
- **ن nun.** the letter that opens surah 68 before the oath by the pen, and the maker's
  fifth body carried an arabic mark before (حُرّ, free). the risk: the terminals the maker
  named in OPEN.md draw it badly, and one arabic letter reads as religious to readers who
  should be met in human terms first.

## the recommendation

one primary mark, ∴, because it says the one sentence the organisation would most want
said about it: what it claims, it can show. one secondary mark, ⧖, kept for a single job:
anything under a sunset carries it. a licence that converts on a date, an exclusivity the
organisation keeps for a stated time and then lets go, a draft with an expiry. so each
mark means one thing, proof and time, and neither has to carry the whole character. the
manifesto can use ∵ and ∴ as the marks on its two halves, the diagnosis and the practice,
which ties the mark to the method instead of to a logo. the prototyping symbol stays on
the table until the maker pastes it.

## what the readers found on the name words

the eight readers were asked to note every verse touching the candidate words. the findings, in
the abdel haleem translation:

- **bayan.** 55:4 "and taught him to communicate" is haleem's rendering of the word; asad and itani
  give the same faculty of clear expression. surah 98 is al-bayyinah, "the clear evidence", and
  75:19 "We shall make it clear" is the last step of a workflow that runs collect, recite, repeat,
  clarify. the refrain "makes His messages clear" runs through the whole book, each time with a
  stated purpose (reflect, understand, guard), so the case for bayan got stronger: it is the
  faculty, the method, and the surah name at once.
- **fitra.** 30:30 "This is the natural disposition God instilled in mankind- there is no altering
  God’s creation". the readers confirmed the word appears there and nowhere else by name; 91:7-8
  and 95:4 carry the idea. it stays the deepest candidate and the least legible.
- **furqan.** 25:1, where haleem coins "the Differentiator": "sent the Differentiator down to His
  servant so that it may be a warning to all people"; 21:48 "distinguishes right from wrong, a light
  and a reminder"; 8:29 the criterion given as a result of conduct. the english coinage is a mark
  nobody else uses, and a strange one, which the brief leaves room for.
- **mizan.** 55:7-9; 42:17 "the Scripture with Truth and the Balance"; 57:25 the balance and iron
  in one shipment; 7:85 and 26:181-182 the shopkeeper's scale. heavy, and exact.
- **burhan.** 2:111, 21:24, 27:64, 28:75 "‘Produce your evidence’" and its kin. the plainest of the
  proof words.
- **qalam.** 68:1 "Nun By the pen! By all they write"; 96:4; 31:27 trees as pens and seven seas of
  ink. the surah named for it, al-qalam, opens with the single letter nun.
- **sanad.** the word itself is absent; the idea is everywhere: 85:3 "by the Witness and that which
  is witnessed", 72:27-28 watchers front and behind to guard a message's delivery, 5:106-108 the
  witness protocol, 2:282 the scribe. the tool's receipt is the closest english word.
- **new candidates the readers surfaced.** raqim, 18:9 "al-Raqim", which itani and sahih give as
  "the Inscription", a written tablet tied to a story of refuge and long sleep: obscure, strange,
  and about a record. khaza'in, the storehouses (12:55, 15:21), where joseph is steward and feeds
  the people who sold him. 'alamat, "landmarks" (16:16), a mark that guides. zabad, the froth
  (13:17). "firm news" (27:22), the hoopoe's verified report. "the surest handhold" (31:22).
  "measure the links well" (34:11), a motto more than a name. "access to tools" came from the human
  side, the whole earth catalog's subtitle, and could sit under any of these.

## what the readers found on the marks

- the hourglass has a cousin in the text: 25:45-46, "Do you not see how your Lord lengthens the
  shade? ... We made the sun its indicator but We gradually draw it towards Us, little by little".
  a sundial. the passing of time is sworn by in 103:1 "By the declining day" and 89:4 "by the
  passing night". the hourglass reading of ⧖ stands; the sundial is the older instrument and the
  gentler one, and "little by little" is also how the text describes its own release (17:106).
- the therefore sign has kin in the form of the text itself: the three-beat "name it, ask what it
  is, define it" (69:1-3 and eleven more places), and the reasoning that ends "Show me your
  evidence then" (27:64). ∵ and ∴ as the marks of the two halves of the manifesto tie the glyph to
  the method and keep it off the logo.
- the text is comfortable with a mark that is not a word: the unexplained letters that open
  twenty-nine surahs ("Alif Lam Mim", "Ya Sin", "Nun", "Ha Mim" as a series mark across seven
  surahs). a single letter as a mark has scriptural precedent, and ن, the letter under the oath by
  the pen, is the one with the most to say. the terminal rendering the maker noted in OPEN.md is
  the practical objection, and it is a real one.
- "twelve springs gushed out, and each group knew its drinking place" (2:60, 7:160) is the image the
  manifesto uses for the public ontology, and it could be drawn: one strike, twelve outlets, no
  toll. it is an illustration, and a mark has to survive at sixteen pixels, so it belongs on the
  page and never in the corner.

## the recommendation, after the reading

unchanged in its shape and firmer in its reasons. keep the name sandbox unless the organisation
renames, and fix the collision at the tool's end. if it renames, bayan first, because 55:4 and
surah 98 make it the faculty, the method and the evidence in one word a mouth can carry; fitra if
the maker wants the deepest word and will pay for the gloss; "the differentiator" if the maker
wants the strangest true one in english. ∴ as the primary mark, ⧖ kept for anything under a
sunset, and ∵ ∴ as the marks on the manifesto's two halves. the prototyping symbol waits on the
codepoint.
