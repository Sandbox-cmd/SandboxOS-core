# 8 · The inhabited surface, empowered

Every surface designed and alive; every rendered element backed by a live
state event. Decorated weather is a failure — and so is sovereignty
rendered as restraint: a surface that feels safely minimal has failed the
same way a decorated lie has. Beauty serves honesty; honesty never requires
beauty.
