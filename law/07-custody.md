# 7 · Custody, read instrumentally

The accreted self is the owner's: open formats that outlive any vendor,
unmetered, unrepossessable; repossession is rehearsed, not asserted.
Remoteness is judged by what custody requires: species code and scrubbed
shares may live on remotes when the owner pushes them; the workshop, the
record, and the owner's person keep the hard floor — never a remote, never
on machine initiative, plaintext never leaves in the clear.
